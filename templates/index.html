GROUNDS STANDARD

GOOD — Grounds are within standard. Turf is serviceable, vegetation is stable, and no intervention is presently needed.

WATCH — A condition is approaching a threshold or deserves another look: growth, moisture stress, ordinary weeds, bare ground, debris, or minor damage.

ACT — Intervention is warranted now or should be routed into the work rhythm.

MOWINGTarget roughly 3–3½ in. Avoid removing more than one-third of the grass blade at once.
WEEDSDistinguish ordinary weeds from suspected noxious weeds. Identification comes before control; observation does not automatically mean chemical treatment.
GROUNDNotice dry or stressed turf, irrigation problems, bare soil, debris, damage, and conditions that may worsen if left unattended.
